\begin{frame}{The gap this project addresses}

Statistical physics models media as an \textbf{external field} $h$ acting on a population of
coupled opinion states.

\vspace{1em}
In that literature the field is \textbf{assigned by hand}. A population is shown to tip,
without establishing that any real influence is strong enough to tip it.

\vspace{1.5em}
\begin{block}{The two questions that follow}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can a field be \emph{measured} from content itself, rather than assumed?
\item How strong would the coupling have to be for a measured spread to matter?
\end{enumerate}
\end{block}

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The instrument}

Text in, predicted cortical response out. Five stages.

\vspace{1em}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Speech synthesis} --- the encoder expects naturalistic audiovisual input.
\item \textbf{Word timings} --- forced alignment recovers per-word timing.
\item \textbf{Embedding} --- text and audio through the pretrained backbones.
\item \textbf{Prediction} --- TRIBE v2 returns $20{,}484$ fsaverage5 cortical vertices per
time point.
\item \textbf{Reduction} --- mean-pooled over time, reduced to two ROI means.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{1em}
Public and running: \texttt{monarch-4iy.pages.dev}

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Three things the released model turned out to be}

Established by inspecting the artifact, not by reading the paper about it.

\vspace{1em}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cortical only.} Config declares \texttt{mesh: fsaverage5}; output is
$(T, 20484) = 2\times 10242$. There is no subcortical head. \textbf{The amygdala is not
predicted}, so the proposal's Eq.~(4) cannot be computed at all.

\vspace{0.6em}
\item \textbf{Averaged over subjects.} The loader forces
\texttt{average\_subjects = True}. The subject term is unavailable.

\vspace{0.6em}
\item \textbf{Output is standardised.} ROI means sit near zero, so a ratio observable
sign-flips or explodes. Undefined for $69$ of $400$ items, $17.25\%$.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.8em}
Each is a property of the instrument, found by testing it.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Reliability: measured, not assumed}

A second GPU session rescanned all $400$ items with identical text, code and regions.

\vspace{0.8em}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
& $r$ & ICC(2,1) & $\mathrm{sd}(\Delta)/\mathrm{sd}_{\text{between}}$ \\
\midrule
$\naa_{\text{signed}}$ & $0.8763$ & $0.8725$ & $0.484$ \\
$A_{\text{aff}}$ & $0.8759$ & $0.8713$ & $0.489$ \\
$A_{\text{del}}$ & $0.8576$ & $0.8550$ & $0.521$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{The separation replicates}: $\eta^2 = 0.0888$ on session two, against $0.1068$ on
session one. Both clear the detectable floor.

\vspace{0.6em}
\textbf{Per-item direction does not.} $51$ of $400$ items, $12.8\%$, reverse sign between
sessions.

\vspace{0.6em}
Group-level claims are supportable. \textbf{No per-item verdict appears anywhere} --- not in
the analysis, the figures, or the public interface.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The physics: mean-field, with coefficients derived}

Self-consistency for a coupled population under field $h$:
\[
m = \tanh(\beta J m + h)
\]

Expanding $\operatorname{artanh}(m) \approx m + m^3/3$ gives the Landau free energy
\[
F(m) = a m^2 + b m^4 - hm, \qquad a = \frac{1 - \beta J}{2}, \quad b = \frac{1}{12}
\]

\vspace{0.8em}
Coefficients \textbf{derived from the self-consistency condition}, not asserted. The proposal
carried $a = 1-\beta J$, $b = (\beta J)^3/3$, which do not follow from it. Corrected.

\vspace{0.8em}
Solver checked against the exact exponents: $\beta = 1/2$, $\gamma = 1$, $\delta = 3$.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The bound, and why no $\hat{\alpha}$ is quoted}

\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\vspace{0.8em}
Calibrating $\alpha$ against the corpus \textbf{does not identify it}: the fit performs worse
than the sample mean out of sample, and the estimate scales with an unmeasured $\beta J$.

\vspace{0.8em}
So no value is quoted. Anywhere. Not in a table, not in a caption.

\vspace{1em}
What survives is a \textbf{necessary condition} on any content observable used as a field:
\[
\alpha \;\ge\; \frac{h_c(\beta J)}{\Delta X}
\]
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{F6_alpha_required.pdf}
\end{column}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.5em}
Measured spread $\Delta X = 0.1241$ gives $\alpha \ge 0.169$ at $\beta J = 1.10$, $1.655$ at
$1.50$, and $4.294$ at $2.00$.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Paper 3: does the encoder track real cortex?}

A commercial lab reports the released checkpoint, in the averaged configuration this project
runs, is \textbf{anti-correlated} with real cortex. Four subjects, one stimulus, not
independently replicated.

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{Measured here}, from public data: inter-subject noise ceiling
$= 0.1517$, CI $[0.1484, 0.1551]$, four subjects, $1000$ of $1000$ Schaefer parcels, episode
\texttt{s01e01a}.

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{A first value of $0.2247$ was withdrawn.} Two silent defects: an episode selected by
position rather than name, and an orientation guard that could not fire. Replaying the old
path reproduces the wrong number exactly, which is what proved the cause.

\vspace{0.8em}
The prediction pass is specified in the paper and is \textbf{on its final run}.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What is claimed, and what is not}

\textbf{Claimed}
\begin{itemize}
\item A content observable that can be measured, with its reliability stated.
\item That it separates these four corpora at $\eta^2 = 0.1068$, power attached.
\item A bound any candidate field observable must satisfy, applicable before data is
collected.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{Not claimed}
\begin{itemize}
\item That the index detects manipulation. Category is confounded with source.
\item Anything about the amygdala. The checkpoint does not predict it.
\item Any value of the coupling $\alpha$. The corpus does not identify it.
\item Any verdict on an individual item. $12.8\%$ reverse between sessions.
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}
